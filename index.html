About Me

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Game Design and Development at the Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT), with an anticipated graduation in May 2027. My coursework includes Game Development Algorithms, Problem Solving, Interactive Media, 2D Animation, and Web Technology.

Along the way, I've earned the RIT Award (merit-based) and received an honorable mention in the China Thinks Big 2019-2020 competition for outstanding research projects. I'm seeking a co-op or internship opportunity for Summer 2025 where I can apply my skills in C# and game development.

Outside of academics, I enjoy exploring new technologies and working on interactive media projects that challenge my problem-solving abilities and creativity.
