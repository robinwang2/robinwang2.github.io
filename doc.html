5. Goal and Audience

The goal of the site is to provide a collection of restaurants that do not require tipping. I believe this will be a comparative advantage for restaurants and encourage store owners to pay more to their employees. The target audience includes individuals who prefer dining at restaurants that do not require tipping and restaurant owners who want to attract such customers. This informed the design choices, focusing on easy navigation, clear presentation of restaurant information, and an overall user-friendly experience.

6. Additional Notes

  • Challenges: One challenging part was optimizing the layout for both desktop and mobile views, ensuring a seamless experience regardless of screen size.
  • Going Above and Beyond: I implemented a responsive grid layout that adjusts beautifully to different screen sizes, along with a carefully considered typography hierarchy to improve readability.
